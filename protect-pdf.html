Frequently asked questions

Are the pages still selectable text?

No. Pages are re-rendered as images before encryption, so text in the protected copy cannot be selected. Always keep a copy of your password.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs inside your browser, so your files never leave your device.
