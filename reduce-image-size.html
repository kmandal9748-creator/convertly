Frequently asked questions

How does the percentage work?

It aims for a file that is that percentage of the original by lowering JPEG quality and, if needed, the image dimensions. PNG and WebP files are saved as JPG.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs inside your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no limit on how many files you process.
